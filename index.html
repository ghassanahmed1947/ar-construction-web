About Us

A Family Business, Built Like Our Houses — To Last

AR Construction started in 2010 with a simple promise: do the job right, on time, and stand behind it. Since then we've completed more than 400 projects in different areas of Karachi — homes built from the foundation up, and interiors reimagined for families already living in them.

We're a family-run company, and we treat every site like it's our own home going up. No shortcuts on material, no disappearing after handover.
